HomeAV · Transparency

Privacy, by design.

Your media is personal. This policy explains what HomeAV processes, how connected services work, and the choices you have.

Effective date: 4 November 2026

1. Overview

HomeAV is a media-library client. Depending on the features you use and your configuration, information may be processed on your device, by a HomeAV server you connect to, by media services you configure, or by an AI provider you select.

HomeAV does not use advertising tracking in the app. The app’s Apple privacy manifest declares that it does not track users and does not declare collected data types. Connected servers and third-party services may nevertheless process information.

2. Information HomeAV may process

  • Account and profile information: usernames, account identifiers, profile names, settings and (where enabled) profile PIN-related authentication data.
  • Authentication: credentials are submitted to your configured HomeAV backend for authentication; session/access and refresh tokens are stored in device Keychain.
  • Media and playback: library titles, artwork/metadata, playback position, progress, favourites, playlists, queue information and preferences.
  • Requests and search: search terms, media requests and messages submitted through request or assistant features.
  • Settings: server addresses, preferences and AI configuration; API keys and session credentials are stored in Keychain where applicable.
  • Support communications: information you choose to include in an email, such as contact details, device/app details and issue descriptions.

HomeAV does not require precise location as a core feature. Avoid including sensitive information in prompts or support messages unless necessary.

3. Where information is sent

Your HomeAV server

When you sign in or use server-connected features, the app communicates with the backend address you configure. That server may process account/profile details, requests, library activity and other information needed to provide its services. The server operator controls hosting, access, retention and security. If you operate the server, you are responsible for its configuration and data practices.

Media services

Services you configure receive requests and credentials/tokens as required by their integrations and operate under their own privacy policies.

Optional AI features

AI features are optional and disabled unless enabled. If you configure OpenAI, Google Gemini or a custom/OpenAI-compatible provider, prompts and relevant library context (potentially including media titles or metadata) are sent to that provider using the API key you supply. The provider processes information under its own terms and privacy policy. A custom endpoint may be local or operated by another party; review its operator and settings before use. Restricted/kids profiles are prevented from using the app’s AI prompt features.

Apple platforms

Apple may process information under its own policies when you use App Store services, device diagnostics or platform functionality. See Apple’s privacy information.

4. Purposes and legal basis

Information is used to operate and secure HomeAV, authenticate accounts, connect media services, maintain playback/profile continuity, respond to support requests, troubleshoot issues and provide optional AI features. Where applicable, processing is based on providing the service you request, legitimate interests in security/support, or your consent/choice for optional features.

5. Sharing, sale and advertising

We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-app advertising tracking. Information is shared with the server and third-party services you choose to connect, as described above, and with support providers only where needed to handle your enquiry. We do not control those services’ privacy practices.

6. Retention and deletion

Local settings and credentials remain on your device until removed, you sign out, reset the app or delete it (some system backups may retain app data). Server-held data is retained under the server operator’s policies. To request deletion of an account or server-held information, contact that server’s operator. For information held by us through support communications, contact us; requests are handled subject to applicable law and legitimate recordkeeping needs.

7. Security

HomeAV uses platform Keychain storage for session credentials and expects secure HTTPS connections for public servers. Security also depends on the servers/services you configure and your device security. Only connect to servers you trust.

8. Children

HomeAV includes profile controls, including restricted/kids profiles. It is not intended to independently collect personal information from children for advertising. Parents/account owners should configure profiles and server permissions appropriately. Contact us if you believe information relating to a child has been provided directly to us.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, or withdraw consent. You can disable AI, disconnect services, clear app data or contact the relevant server operator. Contact us for privacy questions or requests concerning information we control.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as HomeAV changes. The latest version will be published here with a revised effective date.

Privacy questions: homeav@bakatel.co.uk

App Store release checklist: verify this policy against the final app build, backend configuration and SDKs. App Store Connect’s App Privacy answers must independently reflect all data practices.
