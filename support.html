Help us help you

When emailing, it helps to include:

  • Your device (Apple TV, iPhone or iPad) and operating-system version.
  • Your HomeAV app version.
  • What you were trying to do and what happened instead.
  • The exact error message, if one appears.
  • For connection issues, the server/service type and whether the connection check succeeds.
Please do not email passwords, API keys, access tokens or other secret credentials. Redact private server addresses from screenshots.

Quick troubleshooting

Can't connect to a server

Confirm the server is online, use its HomeAV backend address (not a media-service address), check HTTPS/certificate settings, and verify your device can reach it on the network.

Library or playback problems

Check that the configured media service is available and your account/profile has permission to access the item. Try again after reconnecting.

AI assistant issues

AI features require you to enable them and configure a provider, endpoint/model and API key. Check provider availability and usage limits. Prompts and relevant library context may be sent to that provider.

Account or profile questions

If you use a self-hosted HomeAV backend, contact its administrator for account, password, profile or server-side data requests.
